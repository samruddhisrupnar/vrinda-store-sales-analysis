# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end data analytics project focused on evaluating the annual sales performance of **Vrinda Store**. The objective of this project was to analyze historical sales and order data to uncover critical customer trends, assess the effectiveness of various sales channels, identify performance gaps, and deliver data-driven insights aimed at accelerating business growth.

## Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **Data Analytics & Modeling:** Advanced Excel (Power Query, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables)
* **Data Visualization:** Excel Dashboards, Dynamic Charts, Conditional Formatting
* **Methodologies:** Demographic Analysis, KPI Frameworks, Trend & Variance Analysis

## Key Responsibilities & Analytics Workflows

### 1. Data Processing & Management
* Utilized **Advanced Excel** to clean, organize, and restructure raw transactional datasets.
* Validated customer, product, and geographic variables to ensure an audit-ready dataset for downstream reporting.

### 2. Operational & KPI Analysis
* Executed comprehensive **KPI, trend, and variance analysis** to track the store's annual performance milestones.
* Monitored order-status metrics (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned) to isolate fulfillment bottlenecks and performance gaps.

### 3. Customer Demographics & Behavior Segmentation
* Examined customer profile data to identify purchasing patterns across genders, age groups, and geographic locations.
* Correlated demographic data with purchasing frequency to pinpoint the store's highest-value customer segments.

### 4. Sales Channel Optimization
* Analyzed channel-level data (e.g., Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Website) to evaluate relative volume and revenue contributions.
* Identified variations in customer behavior unique to specific platforms to support targeted marketing strategies.

### 5. Reporting & Strategic Insights
* Developed a structured, interactive **Excel Performance Dashboard** to visualize key metrics, trends, and business drivers.
* Translated raw performance patterns into actionable, data-driven recommendations to support executive decision-making.
